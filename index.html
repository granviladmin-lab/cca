Linux Laptop Project

Getting Linux fully working on a 2016 15″ MacBook Pro (A1707): T1 chip, Touch Bar, AMD graphics, Broadcom Wi-Fi.

Shop tech tees
  • doneDesktop: Arch-based Omarchy with the Hyprland tiling window manager
  • doneTouch Bar: revived through the T1 chip, with a custom layout for workspaces and media
  • doneTouch ID: fingerprint unlock for the lock screen and sudo
  • doneSpeakers: working with a patched Cirrus Logic audio driver
  • done5 GHz Wi-Fi: enabled with model-specific Broadcom calibration data
  • doneFaceTime HD camera: bridged to a standard 720p webcam for call apps
  • doneTV casting: Chromecast and Roku over the home network (fix shared upstream)
  • doneNew battery plus a low-power “deep idle” mode when the lid is closed
  • partialSleep: suspend works, but the Touch Bar stays dark until a reboot
  • partialBattery life: the discrete AMD GPU drives the display, so power draw stays high
  • blockedHibernate: the AMD GPU fails to come back from hibernation
  • blockedWi-Fi Direct / Miracast: not supported by the Broadcom Wi-Fi chip

iPad Weather / Security Station

A retired 2013 iPad Air turned into a Mooresville weather display that also watches the room with its back camera. Camera feed is password protected.

View station
  • doneWeather: live conditions, 12-hour and 7-day forecast, and National Weather Service alerts
  • doneMotion camera: the back camera flags movement and saves a snapshot (at most one a minute)
  • doneEncrypted feed: snapshots are AES-256 encrypted on the iPad before upload; only the password opens them
  • doneNo server: runs in Safari on iOS 12, with no app, hub or monthly fee
  • partialInstall: mounting the iPad and setting it to stay awake on its charger
  • blockedNative Linux: Alpine Linux boots on the iPad over USB, but touch, camera and Wi-Fi aren’t supported on its A7 chip
